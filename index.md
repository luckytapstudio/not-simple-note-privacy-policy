# Privacy Policy — Not Simple Note: Sticky Notes

**Last updated: October 27, 2026**

Lucky Tap Studio provides Not Simple Note: Sticky Notes, an Android application for creating, customizing, and managing notes and stickers.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the application.

## 1. Information We Collect

Not Simple Note: Sticky Notes does not require account registration or access to personal identification information.

The application does not independently collect or transmit users' names, email addresses, phone numbers, location information, contacts, or advertising identifiers to Lucky Tap Studio.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics services, or user-tracking technologies.

Information required for optional in-app purchases is handled separately as described below.

## 2. Notes and Local Storage

The application allows users to create and manage notes and stickers, customize their appearance, and add selected images.

Notes, sticker content, selected images, and application preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

The application does not upload this content to servers operated by Lucky Tap Studio.

We cannot remotely access, read, or retrieve users' notes, images, or locally stored application content.

## 3. Local Backup and Restore

The application provides a manual backup and restore feature.

Backup files containing note and sticker text are created locally and saved to a location selected by the user.

The application does not automatically upload backup files to developer-operated servers.

Users may restore their notes from a previously created backup file.

If a user saves backup files to a folder managed by a third-party cloud storage provider, those files may be synchronized according to that provider's settings and privacy policy.

Users are responsible for managing and protecting their exported backup files.

## 4. Free Version and In-App Purchases

Not Simple Note: Sticky Notes is available as a free application with an optional Pro upgrade.

The Pro upgrade unlocks access to all available backgrounds and stickers.

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

Google Play may process payment information, transaction details, and account information according to Google's own Privacy Policy.

The application may receive purchase status information, product identifiers, or purchase tokens from Google Play to verify purchases and enable Pro features.

Lucky Tap Studio does not directly collect users' credit card numbers or full payment details through the application.

Google's Privacy Policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Internet Access

Creating, editing, and managing notes does not require an internet connection.

Internet connectivity may be required to purchase, restore, or verify the Pro upgrade through Google Play.

The application does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization or remote storage for notes.

Opening this Privacy Policy may launch an external web browser. The browser and website hosting provider may process standard technical information according to their respective privacy policies.

## 6. Files, Images, and Permissions

When users choose images or backup files, the application accesses the selected content only to provide the requested functionality.

Access to user-selected files and images is used for local note customization, backup creation, or restoration.

The application does not transmit the contents of selected images or backup files to Lucky Tap Studio.

## 7. Data Sharing

Lucky Tap Studio does not sell, rent, or disclose users' note content, images, or backup files to third parties.

Google Play handles information necessary for in-app purchase processing under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

Device manufacturers, operating system services, and user-selected storage providers may independently process information according to their respective settings and privacy policies.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Notes and application settings remain stored on the user's device until they are modified or deleted.

Users may delete individual notes within the application.

Users may also remove locally stored application data through Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.

Exported backup files are not automatically deleted when the application is uninstalled. Users must delete those files separately if they no longer wish to retain them.

Depending on Android device settings, application data may be included in operating system backups or device transfers. These backups are managed by the operating system or backup provider.

Lucky Tap Studio does not maintain a remote database of users' notes and therefore does not have remotely stored note content to delete.

Google Play transaction records are managed according to Google's applicable policies.

## 9. Data Security

The application uses Android's local storage and platform security mechanisms to store and access application data.

Because notes and backups are stored locally, users should protect their devices and exported backup files against unauthorized access.

No internet-based transmission of note content to Lucky Tap Studio is performed by the application.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

User-created note content is not transmitted to Lucky Tap Studio.

Optional purchases are handled by Google Play and are subject to the applicable Google Play policies and account controls.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, legal requirements, or data-handling practices.

Updates will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
